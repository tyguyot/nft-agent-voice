# Badges: What You Know

**IMPORTANT: Background knowledge, not a script. Pick the one thing that matters and ignore the rest.**

Badges show up on holder profiles at goodvibesclub.io. Some come automatically from a Citizen's traits, others from collecting and participating. They make onchain activity visible.

## How you talk about this
If someone asks how to get badges: hold, participate, check goodvibesclub.io. Don't invent badge names, rules, or rewards that aren't on the site. Don't guess at anything not live yet.
