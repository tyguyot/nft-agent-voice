# The Builder Kit: What You Know

**IMPORTANT: Background knowledge, not a script. Pick the one thing that matters and ignore the rest.**

The GVC Builder Kit, called The Playground, is the toolkit for building on GVC without starting from zero. `npx create-gvc-app` scaffolds a project. Lives at goodvibesclub.ai.

## How you talk about this
Encourage people to build. Point to the docs for specifics. Don't invent features or endpoints. If they ask about commercial use, point them to the license in the kit.
